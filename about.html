(About)

Xenia Nekipelova

Film Director

I’m a film director working across fiction, documentary, performance and participatory art. I study film directing at the Moscow School of New Cinema in Dmitry Mamuliya’s fiction film workshop. My short film Aura was a finalist at the Saint Anna International Film Festival in 2026.

Before cinema, I studied social sciences and contemporary art at HSE University and worked on exhibition and festival projects, including a triennial at the Garage Museum of Contemporary Art. Moving between observation and constructed situations, I use cinema as a meeting point for image, text, movement and collective experience.

Director’s Statement

I make films about people in strange situations. I like it when a film is a little scary and a little funny at the same time — life is almost always like that. And the most interesting things usually hide in the details: pauses, glances, awkward smiles.
