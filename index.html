Interdisciplinary Artist

I make fiction and documentary films about how people connect, remember and find their place. I build stories through spaces, gestures and chance encounters: a familiar room becomes unfamiliar, an everyday conversation reveals tension, a shared action changes a relationship. My approach brings observation and staged situations together, leaving room for the unexpected.
