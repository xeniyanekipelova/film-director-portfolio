Two young musicians live and make music in home studios, where the boundary between life and creativity dissolves. For her, music grows out of impulse and becomes a way of processing emotions; for him, it offers a way to understand himself and stay connected to others. Observing their everyday lives, the film gradually becomes a reflection on the inner need to create — regardless of recognition, success or the number of listeners.
